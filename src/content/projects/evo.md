The idea came from people setting their New Year's resolutions and wanting a way to stick to them. Evo: Grow Daily is a mix of BeReal and Strava for habits and goals: like BeReal, you post a photo every day as proof that you showed up; like Strava, your friends follow your progress, cheer you on and compete with you on a leaderboard. There are also communities for people with similar goals.

I built it in Expo and React Native with Supabase behind it, starting in November 2025, and it went live on the App Store on 31 December 2025, in time for the new year. It's still listed there, but the project is paused and its database is shut down, so the app doesn't work right now.
