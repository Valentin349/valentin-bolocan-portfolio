Poise lists singles and dating events run by independent hosts. You pick your city, browse what's on, pick a night and book with the host on their own page. There are no profiles to swipe, no messages and no matching: you meet people in the room.

Poise went live on the App Store on 11 June 2026 as a deck of five-minute social challenges with short lessons. In September 2026 it became an events app: the challenges are still there as the optional part, and the events are the product.

The app is built with Expo and React Native on Supabase.
