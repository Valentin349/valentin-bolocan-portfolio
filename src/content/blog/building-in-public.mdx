Building in public has completely changed how I approach projects. Instead of working in isolation until something is "perfect," I've started sharing early and often.

## The Fear of Judgment

At first, it felt uncomfortable. What if people thought my ideas were silly? What if my code wasn't good enough? But I realized that perfectionism was just another form of procrastination.

## What Changed

- **Accountability**: Sharing progress publicly kept me motivated
- **Feedback loops**: Early input helped me avoid costly mistakes
- **Community**: I connected with people facing similar challenges
- **Learning**: Teaching others solidified my own understanding

## The Reality

Most people don't care about your half-finished projects as much as you think. And the ones who do care are usually supportive and helpful. The fear exists mostly in your head.

Now I share experiments, abandoned projects, and failures alongside my successes. It's liberating.
