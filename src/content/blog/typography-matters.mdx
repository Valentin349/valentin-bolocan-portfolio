Good typography is invisible. Bad typography is everywhere.

## The Fundamentals

Typography isn't just about choosing a pretty font. It's about:

1. **Readability**: Can people actually read your content comfortably?
2. **Hierarchy**: Does the visual structure guide readers through your content?
3. **Spacing**: Are you giving your text room to breathe?

## Line Length Matters

The ideal line length is 60-70 characters. Too long, and readers' eyes get tired tracking across the screen. Too short, and reading becomes choppy.

## Whitespace is Not Wasted Space

Generous spacing between sections helps readers process information. Dense layouts feel overwhelming and get ignored.

## Font Pairing

Stick to one or two fonts. Use weight and size to create hierarchy instead of introducing multiple typefaces. Inter at different weights is often all you need.

The best design is barely noticed. But its absence is felt immediately.
